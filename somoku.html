Somoku

Somoku Series began with photographs, during a trip I took alone. On the last day of my trip to Damyang, a town known for bamboo, I was struck by the soft rain, the waving leaves, and the landscape filled with green. I wanted viewers to experience the scene and feeling I had at that moment through my work in an indirect way. I focused on the forms of bamboo, raindrops, and the way the leaves overlapped as they moved in the wind.

Rather than using glass as a substitute for gemstones, I explored expressions and joining structures that make use of the properties of glass.
